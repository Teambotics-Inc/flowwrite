One document. Two ways to read it.

01 / The checks

Reading at
two
resolutions.

Strip

Read just the headings and anchors. Does the argument still hold, including important qualifications?

Naturalness

Remove the emphasis. Does the full text still read naturally?

Attention

Does every anchor carry a claim, distinction, or decision that’s worth noticing?

02 / Use it

Try it on
real writing.

Paste the instruction into your assistant and give it a draft that has a decision, a reason, and a caveat. Then ask someone to read only the emphasized parts and tell you what they understood.

Use FlowWrite anywhere text appears: in a chat, on your website, or in a document.

Your agent uses the cues the channel supports: bold, italics, or underlining where available. In plain text, short labels and line breaks can do the same job.

Agent instruction / v0.1 starter
Follow FlowWrite: write natural full-resolution prose while embedding a sparse secondary pathway of emphasized semantic anchors. A reader following primarily those anchors should be able to reconstruct the core through-line without being misled by missing context. Emphasize meaning-bearing claims and relationships rather than keywords. Preserve material uncertainty and qualifications. Before responding, mentally strip away the ordinary prose and verify that the remaining headings and anchors communicate the essential argument; then verify that the complete prose still reads naturally without the formatting.
